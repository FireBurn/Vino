## Evidence boundary

The Windows captures establish framing and the DL7400 topology, not usable Linux decryption keys.
Keys/tokens are ephemeral and the Linux DisplayLinkManager's current key schedule is still unknown.

| Connector | Video sub | Sealed stream-open sub | Endpoint |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | `0x00` | `0x07` | `0x08` |
| 1 | `0x08` | `0x0f` | `0x0a` |
| 2 | `0x10` | `0x17` | `0x08` |
| 3 | `0x18` | `0x1f` | `0x0a` |

The observed stream-open plaintext structure is documented in `docs/navarro.md`; its final opaque
u16 token is not known. Do not enable `force_video`, guess a token, or claim HDR metadata/codec
support before a differential capture proves it. `docs/hdr.md` is the source of truth for this
boundary.
