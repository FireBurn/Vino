The remaining protocol work is offline only. A stored rr trace of `DisplayLinkManager` is being
replayed in a private mount namespace, never started against the live dock. Reconstructed trace-era
`libstdc++` and `libgcc`, plus a disposable local RR recovery build, advance past the initial stale
`syscall_bp_vm` breakpoint and reach global event 47049. There, RR's scheduler breakpoint bookkeeping
turns into an unrecorded `SIGILL` at DLM module offset `0x26b385`; advancing by suppressing it would
no longer be a trustworthy replay. The recorded snapshot at event 47000 remains usable for static
inspection, but it has not yielded a factory invocation or the writer that performs the authentic
key derivation. It does contain three already-constructed final sealers: each has two AES-128
expanded-key contexts, and static inspection proves that the first 16 bytes at context `+0x10` are
the seed copied into the AES expansion routine. This is post-derivation snapshot evidence only; it
does not associate a sealer with a wire stream or disclose the opaque stream-opening token. Keep the
Vino video gate closed until a trustworthy replay or equivalent evidence establishes that mapping.

The documented DLM 3.4.26 sealer factory is module-relative `0x86cca0` (runtime
`0x55809973fca0` in this trace), with the input key at `rdx+0x18` and riv at `rdx+0x30`. A breakpoint
there did not fire before the event-47049 replay boundary. The temporary recovery RR tree under
`/tmp/rr-bp-recover` is an extraction aid only; do not install, commit, or use it for ordinary RR
work.

Static control flow identifies the final sealer's three usable virtual operations. Its data transform
at `0x85c990` passes the first expanded AES context and the RIV/counter subobject into the software
block core at `0x1cf370`; it also snapshots the counter at outer-object `+0x40`. Its companion at
`0x85ca10` uses the second expanded context to produce the 16-byte Dl3Cmac-style tag: its callee
byte-swaps the supplied counter into the RIV-derived input, chains AES blocks, and applies the
`0x80` partial-block padding. This independently supports Vino's existing AES-CTR plus Dl3Cmac
construction. The constructor stores the factory selector as a big-endian u32 at outer-object
`+0x38`; the three event-47000 sealers therefore map to wire subs `0x04`, `0x08`, and `0x09`.
Each reference is a factory-result wrapper whose static vtable is `0xa73678`, with the caller's
configuration pointer at `+0x08` and the sealer at `+0x10`, exactly as factory `0x86cca0` builds
it. The three wrappers reside in different allocator regions, and their same-layout configuration
objects independently store the corresponding selector at `+0x30`. The nearby
`ManagementProtocolIoImpl` literal is a separate allocation and is not ownership evidence. The
higher-level message and owning connector for all three sealers remain unclassified.
